Ver 1.500: Scan now checks multiple locations, retains usable elevation sources when another sample fails, and warns about isolated terrain tiles with filenames and coordinates. SCO LIDEX now processes large-route OSM and PolyVeg data in stages. Intermediate geometry and section fragments are stored in a temporary spatially indexed GeoPackage; exclusions and visible surfaces are processed per terrain section, GeoJSON validation and cache checks stream one feature at a time, and map rendering loads only the geometry needed for each tile. This addresses the memory exhaustion reported during validation of million-feature exclusion files. Empty or negligible-area exports are skipped with source diagnostics; hardware, memory, and detailed failure reports improve troubleshooting. The established four-file TSRE PolyVeg handoff, source identities, colors, holes, and vegetation clearance are preserved. Temporary staging uses disk space under the route osm_data folder. SCO LIDEX uses USGS 1m, Original Product Resolution 5m~, NED 10m, and key-free Copernicus GLO-30 fallback for normal terrain; Distant Mountains use Copernicus GLO-30 exclusively. It supports route tiles, text lists, marker files, KML files, and track databases; creates optional OSM/PolyVeg map data from anonymous Geofabrik extracts; provides read-only Scan, Append and Overwrite modes, terrain-resolution conversion, bounded rolling seam processing, retry reporting, and automatic cache reuse. Windows 10 or newer and internet access are required when an uncached source is needed. Always back up routes before use. Issued under GNU GPL v3 or later. By Scott Brunner, Beast of Burden.
