SCO LIDEX Known Issues
======================

- Experimental 4m export is deactivated. Its GUI control remains disabled and
  normal builds produce only the supported 8m terrain format. The test mechanism
  remains in source pending renewed Open Rails format work.

- USGS services may occasionally time out or fail for specific areas. Retry
  later or use the failed-tile list with Use Text File + Append.

- Routes outside USGS elevation coverage will not build useful terrain.

- Clean Tile Wipe is destructive. It may remove tile-specific texture, water,
  overlay, or other TSRE edits.

- KML polygon handling is basic. Lines and coordinate paths are sampled, but
  full polygon fill should be considered experimental.

- Distant mountain appearance may vary between TSRE and Open Rails views.

- Existing DEMEX-style distant mountain tiles are replaced during Run when
  Create DM Tiles is selected.

- Very large routes can take a long time. Start with Append or a small
  SCOLIDEXTiles.txt test list when validating a new route.

- Scan Override bypasses preflight checks. It is intended for advanced retry or
  known-good cases, not first-time route validation.
